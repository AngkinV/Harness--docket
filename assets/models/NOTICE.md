# 小机器人 / RobotExpressive

Model by Tomás Laulhé (Quaternius): https://www.patreon.com/quaternius
Three.js version includes facial expression targets, FBX2GLTF conversion and material cleanup by Don McCurdy: https://donmccurdy.com/

Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/RobotExpressive
License: CC0 1.0 Universal — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The distributed derivative normalizes skin weights. The source checksum, derivative checksum and size are recorded in manifest.json. No textures are referenced (0 bytes decoded texture data). All fourteen original animations are retained; Idle is the default.
